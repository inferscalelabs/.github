# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**InferScale Labs**
**Effective date:** November 17, 2026
**Last updated:** November 17, 2026

This Refund and Cancellation Policy ("Policy") explains how cancellations, refunds, and billing adjustments work for services and products provided by InferScale Labs ("InferScale Labs", "we", "us", or "our"). It applies to all clients and customers ("you") who engage our services or purchase our products through [inferscalelabs.com](https://inferscalelabs.com) or under a signed agreement with us.

Where a signed Master Services Agreement (MSA), Statement of Work (SOW), proposal, or order form contains different terms, **those signed terms take precedence** over this Policy for that engagement.

---

## 1. Scope

This Policy covers:

- **Project engagements** — fixed-scope builds such as 0-to-1 MVP development, AI pipeline development, and backend engineering.
- **Time-and-materials and retainer engagements** — ongoing engineering capacity billed hourly, daily, or monthly.
- **Advisory services** — Fractional CTO advisory, architectural due diligence, cloud cost audits, and scalability roadmaps.
- **Software subscriptions** — SaaS tools and products developed and operated by InferScale Labs.

---

## 2. Project Engagements (Fixed Scope)

### 2.1 Deposits

Fixed-scope projects typically require an upfront deposit before work begins. The deposit secures engineering capacity, covers discovery, planning, and architecture work, and is **non-refundable once work has commenced**, except as described in Section 2.3.

### 2.2 Cancellation by Client

You may cancel a fixed-scope project at any time by giving written notice to [contact@inferscalelabs.com](mailto:contact@inferscalelabs.com). On cancellation:

- You will be invoiced for all work completed up to the effective cancellation date, including completed milestones and work in progress on the current milestone, calculated on a pro-rata basis.
- You will be invoiced for any non-cancellable third-party costs incurred on your behalf (for example, licenses, cloud infrastructure, or API usage).
- Any amounts you have prepaid in excess of the above will be refunded.
- Upon full payment of amounts owed, we will deliver all work product completed up to the cancellation date, as provided in your agreement.

### 2.3 Cancellation Before Work Begins

If you cancel in writing **before any work has started** on the project, we will refund your deposit minus any non-refundable third-party costs already incurred and any administrative fees stated in your proposal or SOW.

### 2.4 Completed Milestones

Payments for milestones that have been delivered and accepted (or deemed accepted under the terms of your agreement) are **non-refundable**. If a deliverable does not meet the agreed acceptance criteria, our remedy is to correct the deliverable within a reasonable time, as described in Section 6.

---

## 3. Time-and-Materials and Retainer Engagements

### 3.1 Notice Period

Unless your agreement specifies otherwise, either party may end a time-and-materials or retainer engagement with **14 days' written notice**. Work performed during the notice period is billable.

### 3.2 Unused Retainer Hours

- Retainer hours are allocated per billing period and **do not roll over** unless your agreement states otherwise.
- Unused hours in a billing period that has already started are not refundable.
- If you cancel with proper notice, any prepaid retainer for billing periods that have **not yet started** will be refunded in full.

### 3.3 Hourly and Daily Work

Time already worked is billable and non-refundable. Time is tracked and reported as described in your agreement.

---

## 4. Advisory Services

### 4.1 Scheduled Sessions

Advisory sessions, workshops, and calls can be rescheduled or cancelled free of charge with at least **48 hours' notice**. Sessions cancelled with less than 48 hours' notice, or missed without notice, may be charged in full.

### 4.2 Audits and Assessments

For fixed-fee audits and assessments (such as architectural due diligence or cloud cost audits):

- Cancellation **before** the engagement begins: full refund, less any non-refundable costs incurred.
- Cancellation **after** the engagement begins: you will be charged for work completed to date on a pro-rata basis, and any remaining prepaid balance will be refunded.
- Once the final report or deliverable has been delivered, the fee is **non-refundable**.

---

## 5. Software Subscriptions (SaaS)

### 5.1 Cancelling a Subscription

You can cancel a subscription at any time from your account settings or by contacting us. Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current billing period**, and you will keep access to paid features until then. You will not be charged for subsequent billing periods.

### 5.2 Refunds

- **Monthly plans:** Payments are non-refundable. Cancelling stops future charges.
- **Annual plans:** If you cancel within **14 days** of the initial purchase or renewal charge, you may request a full refund. After 14 days, annual payments are non-refundable, but you keep access until the end of the paid term.
- **Free trials:** If your plan includes a free trial, you will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

### 5.3 Plan Changes

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately; you will be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the start of the next billing period. No partial refunds or credits are issued for downgrades mid-period.

### 5.4 Service Outages

If a product is materially unavailable due to issues on our side, we may, at our discretion or as described in any applicable Service Level Agreement, provide service credits. Service credits are applied to future invoices and are not paid out in cash.

---

## 6. Defective or Non-Conforming Work

If a deliverable does not meet the acceptance criteria agreed in your SOW, please notify us in writing within the acceptance period stated in your agreement (or within **14 days of delivery** if none is stated), describing the issue in reasonable detail. We will correct the deliverable at no additional cost. If we are unable to correct it within a reasonable time, we will work with you in good faith on an appropriate remedy, which may include a partial refund for the affected milestone.

---

## 7. Cancellation by InferScale Labs

We may cancel or suspend an engagement or subscription if:

- invoices remain unpaid after their due date and any applicable grace period;
- you materially breach your agreement or our terms of service; or
- we are unable to continue for reasons outside our reasonable control.

If we cancel for reasons that are **not** caused by your breach or non-payment, we will refund any prepaid amounts for work not yet performed or subscription periods not yet used. Payment for work already performed remains due.

---

## 8. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

Send your request to **[contact@inferscalelabs.com](mailto:contact@inferscalelabs.com)** with:

1. Your name and company name.
2. The project, engagement, or subscription concerned.
3. The relevant invoice or order number.
4. The reason for your request (optional, but it helps us improve).

We aim to acknowledge requests within **2 business days** and to resolve them within **10 business days**.

---

## 9. Refund Processing

- Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method** unless otherwise agreed in writing.
- Refunds are processed within **7–10 business days** of approval. The time for funds to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.
- Refunds are issued in the currency of the original payment. We are not responsible for exchange-rate differences or fees charged by your bank or payment provider.
- Taxes are refunded only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

---

## 10. Chargebacks

If you have a concern about a charge, please contact us first so we can resolve it directly. Initiating a chargeback without first contacting us may result in suspension of services or accounts while the dispute is investigated.
